FAQ

Common questions

Do I need an appointment to visit the showroom?

No. Walk in any time during opening hours. If you'd like a specific car ready for a test drive, book ahead and we'll have it waiting for you.

Can I get a test drive at home?

Yes, we offer free doorstep test drives within city limits. Choose "Test drive" in the form above or call our sales team.

How fast can my car loan be approved?

With complete documents, most loans are approved within 30 minutes to a few hours through our partner banks.

Do you accept my old car in exchange?

Absolutely. Bring your car for a free on-the-spot valuation, and the value is adjusted directly against your new purchase.

How quickly will you reply to my message?

We respond to all enquiries within 24 hours, and usually much sooner during opening hours. For urgent help, call or WhatsApp us.
